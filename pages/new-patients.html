What having a family GP means

A family GP gets to know you and your family over time. Registering with GP4U Clinic in Newbridge, Co. Kildare gives you a practice to come back to for ongoing care, not just one-off visits:

  • Care for babies, children and adults in one practice
  • Ongoing care and reviews for long-term conditions such as asthma, diabetes and blood pressure
  • One record across your walk-in and online visits
  • Prescriptions, certificates and referral letters where your GP considers them appropriate

After you register, our team reviews your details and gets in touch. See the GP services we offer, our fees, or questions about registering. Need to be seen before then? The walk-in clinic is open to everyone — no registration needed.

Questions about registering

Can I register my children and family?

Yes. Add each family member (name, date of birth and relationship) at the bottom of the form. You can share their health details when you first see us.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our team reviews your details and gets in touch. You'll also receive a confirmation email with your reference number.

Need to be seen today?

You don't need to register online first — check in online for our walk-in clinic or just come in. If it's an emergency, call 112 or 999.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Your address and health details are encrypted at rest, and only our clinical team can view them. See our Privacy & GDPR Notice.
