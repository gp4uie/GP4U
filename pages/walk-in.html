A walk-in GP clinic in Newbridge, Co. Kildare

GP4U Clinic is a walk-in GP clinic and family practice in Newbridge. You don't need an appointment and you don't need to be registered with us — adults and children can come in during opening hours and see a GP. Waiting times vary depending on how busy we are.

Looking for ongoing care for you and your family? You can also register with our family GP practice. Can't get to the clinic? See a GP online by video or phone.

Walk-in is a good choice for

  • Coughs, colds, flu and chest infections
  • Sore throats and ear problems
  • Urine infections
  • Rashes and skin infections
  • Minor injuries and sprains
  • Stomach upsets and vomiting
  • Sick certificates and medical letters

What to bring

A list of any medicines you take, and anything relevant such as recent test results or letters from other doctors.

When to go to A&E instead

Call 112 or 999, or go to your nearest Emergency Department, if you or someone else has:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Signs of a stroke — face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Heavy bleeding that won't stop
  • A seizure or loss of consciousness
  • A severe allergic reaction
  • Thoughts of harming yourself — call 112 or 999, or Samaritans on 116 123

Walk-in clinic questions

Common questions about the walk-in clinic

Where is the GP4U walk-in clinic?

Our walk-in clinic is in Newbridge, Co. Kildare. Opening hours and how to reach us are on our walk-in clinic page and contact page.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment is required. You can simply walk in during opening hours. Checking in online lets us know you're on your way and helps us prepare for your visit, but it does not reserve a specific appointment time. You can check in online if you'd like to.

How does the walk-in clinic work?

Walk in during opening hours, check in at reception, and see a GP. If you'd like, you can check in online first so we know you're on your way. Waiting times vary depending on how busy we are.

When is the walk-in clinic open?

We're open seven days a week. Hours may differ on public holidays.

How long will I wait?

Waiting times vary depending on how busy we are. Checking in online lets us know you're coming, but it doesn't reserve a set appointment time.

What should I bring to the clinic?

A list of any medicines you take, and anything relevant such as recent test results or letters from other doctors.

Do I need to register to use the walk-in clinic?

No. Anyone can use the walk-in clinic without registering. Registering with our family practice is for ongoing care — for you and your family, including reviews of long-term conditions and follow-up — rather than one-off visits.

Do you see children?

Our family practice cares for children as well as adults, and children can be brought to the walk-in clinic. For an online consultation for a child, please contact us first.

How much does a consultation cost?

Online consultation prices are shown on our Fees page and again before you pay. For walk-in and family practice fees, please contact us or ask at reception.

Can I get a medical certificate?

Yes, where your GP considers it appropriate. You can request a sick certificate through our online booking, or ask your GP during a walk-in visit. Your GP can also write medical letters where clinically appropriate.
