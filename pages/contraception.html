Contraceptive Pill, Patch & Ring

If you're already established on a contraceptive pill, patch, or ring and need to continue it, our GPs can review your history online and issue a prescription without an in-person appointment.

Book online — €25.00
This may not be suitable for you if…
  • Possible current pregnancy
  • You smoke and are aged 35 or over
  • History of blood clots (DVT/PE), or a strong family history
  • History of heart attack, stroke, or angina
  • Migraine with aura
  • Uncontrolled high blood pressure
  • Breastfeeding a baby under 6 weeks old
  • History of breast cancer or another hormone-sensitive cancer
  • Liver disease
  • Bariatric surgery or a malabsorption condition
  • Significant side effects from current contraceptive — needs assessment

If any of these apply to you, book a Video Consultation instead so your GP can assess you properly — the booking form will offer to switch you over automatically if needed.

How your prescription reaches your pharmacy: if your GP approves your request, they email your prescription directly to the pharmacy you name during booking.

About contraception

Hormonal contraception (the pill, patch, or ring) is a safe, effective, and widely used way to prevent pregnancy for most women, with several types available depending on your health history and preference.

The right method depends on individual factors — some options aren't suitable with certain migraine types, blood clot history, smoking status, or other conditions, which is exactly what the safety questionnaire is designed to check before a written renewal.

Online review suits continuing a contraceptive method you're already using and tolerating well. Starting a new method for the first time, or significant side effects, are often better discussed over video.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch to a different pill online?

Continuing your current method is the most straightforward request — switching methods is often better discussed over video so your GP can talk through the options.

What if I’m having side effects?

Mention this in your booking — significant side effects may mean a video consultation is more appropriate than a written renewal.

Does this cover emergency contraception?

No — emergency contraception is time-sensitive; contact a pharmacy directly or book a same-day video consultation.

Do I need a smear test before renewing?

Not for a routine contraception renewal, but keep your cervical screening up to date separately through the National Screening Service.
