# 90-day launch plan (from the week of 28 Sep 2026)

Owners: **F** = founder/owner · **GP** = GP(s) on camera / clinical reviewer · **M** = marketing (you, a freelancer or agency) · **Dev** = website.
Effort is rough hours for the week. Success metrics are for decisions, not vanity.

| **3 — Content launch** | Publish posts 1–10 of [first-30-posts](../social/first-30-posts.md); film first 6 short videos (one 2-hour session); Welcome email sequence live | M, GP | first-30-posts, video scripts, email templates | 10–12h | GP filming availability; clinical review of scripts | Posting on schedule; saves/shares on education posts |

| **11–12 — Scale & review** | Quarter review: channel mix, budget scenario for Q2, content gaps from search terms; plan clinic Phase 3 run-in if a date is confirmed | F, M | dashboard | 6–8h | 12 weeks' data | Decision made: stay Lean / move to Growth; next-quarter plan written |

dashboard check (Monday).
