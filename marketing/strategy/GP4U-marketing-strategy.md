# GP4U marketing & growth strategy — 12 months, with a 90-day launch

*Version 1 · 24 September 2026 · Owner: GP4U founder · Status: Phase 1 (online GP launch)*

> **Known facts vs assumptions.** Facts in this document come from the GP4U website and admin settings (services, live
> prices, "online GP 7 days", clinic in Newbridge not yet open) or from public competitor websites checked on
> 24 Sep 2026. Everything about audience size, costs per click/booking and growth rates is a **planning assumption** to
> be replaced with GP4U's own data after the first 4–6 weeks. Nothing here guarantees a number of patients.

---

## 1. Brand positioning

**Positioning statement.** For people in Ireland who need a GP but can't easily get to one when they need it, GP4U is
a GP service run by real Irish-registered doctors that makes getting care as simple as one tap — online now, and soon in
person at our Newbridge clinic. Unlike faceless prescription websites, GP4U is a human, local, doctor-led practice that
happens to be easy to use.

- **Primary line:** One tap. Real care.
- **Supporting line:** Online GP care from wherever you are in Ireland.
- **Brand idea:** Real doctors. Real care. Made easier.
- **Clinic line (Phase 2–3):** GP care when you need it.
- **Proof we can show today:** Irish-registered GPs; video or phone; 7 days; prices shown before you book; secure,
  encrypted records; prescriptions sent to the pharmacy you choose where appropriate; honest about when you need to be
  seen in person.
- **Proof we must build:** named doctors with photos and Medical Council numbers; real clinic; genuine reviews;
  press coverage.
- **Never claim:** "leading", "best", "fastest", "No.1", "guaranteed prescription", "same-day" (unless operationally
  guaranteed), outcomes, or that online care suits every problem.

## 3. Value propositions

1. **Convenience** — book in minutes, video or phone, from anywhere in Ireland, 7 days.
2. **Real GPs** — Irish-registered doctors; a person, not a chatbot.
3. **Clarity** — prices shown before you book; what happens next explained.
4. **Sensible care** — prescriptions, sick certs and referral letters where your GP considers them appropriate; honest
   when you need to be seen in person.
5. **Privacy** — encrypted records, secure video, GDPR-first.
6. **Local roots** — a real Newbridge clinic coming soon (Phase 2+), so online care has a physical home.

## 5. Competitor analysis (desk research, 24 Sep 2026)

| Competitor | Positioning (public site) | Notes for GP4U |
|---|---|---|
| Webdoctor.ie | Scale & awards: "leading online doctor", patient numbers, Trustpilot, media logos; now also an in-person Dublin clinic; video from ~€39, prescriptions from ~€25 | Online + physical is **not unique** — GP4U's difference must be *local Kildare roots and a human, doctor-first voice*, not the hybrid model itself |
| GPLine.ie | "See an IMC-registered GP online — today"; publishes doctor profiles with IMC numbers; €45 consult, €30 sick cert | Doctor profiles with IMC numbers are a strong trust signal GP4U should match |
| GP24.ie | 24/7 availability | Don't compete on hours we can't match; compete on warmth and clarity |
| Doxonline.ie, GPDoctor.ie, DocOnCall.ie, SmartScripts.ie, Eirdoc, HealthHero.ie | Price-led ("from €30/€34.99", "prescription in 2 hours €20"); HealthHero also via insurers | Price-led market: GP4U should be **clearly priced, not cheapest** (premium but fair). Avoid a race to the bottom |
| Newbridge GPs (e.g. Curragh Grange Family Practice, Moorefield, Newbridge Medical – Centric Health; Doctor 365 walk-in listed in directories) | Established local practices; at least one walk-in service already listed | The clinic launch must earn trust locally: real team, clear hours, clear fees, community presence. Verify each competitor's current offer before launch |

**Observed market patterns:** heavy price anchoring; "same day" and "24/7" claims; Trustpilot badges; condition-page
SEO for prescriptions; little genuine personality or local identity; few show the actual doctors.

**GP4U's openings**
1. **Human brand** — show the real GPs (names, faces, Medical Council numbers) in content, not just badges.
2. **Irish warmth + clinical calm** — plain-English education that isn't selling.
3. **Online now + local clinic soon** — the only service building a Kildare base in public (the renovation story).
4. **Clear patient journey** — "what happens next" at every step; honest about limits.
5. **Premium presentation** — photography and design quality above the price-led players.

## 6. Social strategy

- **Priority:** Instagram + Facebook + TikTok (Phase 1). LinkedIn for credibility (1–2/week). YouTube for evergreen
  explainers (2–4/month).
- **Cadence:** Instagram 3–5/week, Facebook 3–5/week, TikTok 2–4/week, LinkedIn 1–2/week, YouTube 2–4/month. Quality first.
- **Pillars (8):** Meet GP4U · GP education · Online GP explained · Behind GP4U · Patient convenience · Myth vs fact ·
  Future clinic (Phase 2) · Brand stories. Details: [../social/content-pillars.md](../social/content-pillars.md).
- **Face of the brand:** a real GP on camera is the single biggest trust lever. If doctors can't appear yet, lead with
  the founder's voice over clean graphics and b-roll — never actors presented as GP4U doctors.
- **Community management:** reply within one working day; never discuss anyone's personal medical situation in public —
  move to "please book a consultation or email admin@gp4u.ie" (and never ask for health details by DM).
- Files: [profiles](../social/), [90-day calendar](../social/calendar-90-days.csv), [first 30 posts](../social/first-30-posts.md),

## 7. Content strategy

- **Website/blog:** 2 clinically reviewed articles a month for 6 months (12 total), prioritised by patient questions and
  search demand. Plan and briefs: [../content/content-plan-6-months.md](../content/content-plan-6-months.md).
- **Repurposing chain:** one reviewed article → 1 YouTube explainer → 3 short videos → 1 carousel → 1 email section → 1
  LinkedIn post. Clinical review happens once, at article stage.
- **Health info section** (`/blog.html`) is currently hidden and noindexed because its posts are seed text — relaunch
  it only with GP-reviewed articles showing author/reviewer and "last reviewed" date.

## 8. Paid advertising

- **Google Search first** (high intent). Brand + "online GP / online doctor Ireland" + consultation-type ad groups.
  Build: [../google-ads/](../google-ads/).
- **Meta** (Facebook/Instagram): awareness and education video, then retargeting of general-page visitors, then
  conversion once the pixel has consented data. Build: [../meta-ads/](../meta-ads/).
- **TikTok:** Spark Ads boosting organic GP videos that already perform. No hard-sell creative.
- **Hard rules:** no ads naming prescription-only medicines (Irish advertising rules); no targeting by health
  condition; no condition-page retargeting; ad pixels only on general pages (already enforced by the website).
- **Before spending on prescription-related keywords:** check Google's healthcare & medicines policy for Ireland
  (telemedicine/online pharmacy certification may apply) — flagged for review.

## 9. SEO integration

The site's technical SEO is done (clean URLs, schema, sitemap, crawlable content). Growth now comes from:
- Content (section 7) targeting "online GP Ireland" questions and condition questions — each linking to `/online-gp/`.
- Trust signals on the site: named doctors + Medical Council numbers on About; reviewer names on articles.
- Links and mentions: PR (section 11), Irish health/business directories, local Newbridge organisations (Phase 3).
- Phase 3: Google Business Profile + address + local schema (switch-on is built in — see clinic-launch).
- Track in Search Console: impressions/clicks for "online GP", branded "GP4U" searches, Newbridge queries.

## 20. Compliance checklist

Every item of marketing passes [../compliance/compliance-checklist.md](../compliance/compliance-checklist.md) before
publishing. Key sources to check (not legal advice — items marked **REVIEW** need a professional):
- Medical Council *Guide to Professional Conduct and Ethics* (9th edition, 2024): information must be factually accurate,
  evidence-based, verifiable and not raise unrealistic expectations. **REVIEW** its guidance on testimonials and
  advertising before using any patient quotes.
- Advertising prescription-only medicines to the public is restricted in Ireland (Medicinal Products advertising rules /
  HPRA). **REVIEW** before any content that names a medicine.
- ASAI Code (advertising standards), GDPR + ePrivacy (cookies, email consent), Data Protection Commission guidance.
- Platform rules: Google Healthcare & medicines policy; Meta advertising standards (no targeting/assuming health
  conditions; health & wellness restrictions); TikTok ad policies (healthcare).
- Children's health content: general information only, clinically reviewed, never directed at children.
