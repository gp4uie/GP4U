# TikTok ads — Phase 1

Keep TikTok native: boost what already works organically (**Spark Ads**) rather than making "ads".

| Campaign | Objective | When | Audience | Creative | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `TT_Spark_Awareness` | Reach / Video views | Week 7+ | Ireland 18+ broad (never under 18) | Top 2 organic GP videos by watch-through and saves | — |
| `TT_Spark_Traffic` | Website traffic | Once the pixel has consented data | Ireland 18+ broad + video engagers | Same videos + "Book online" CTA | `/online-gp/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=online_gp_launch&utm_content=spark_v03` |
| `TT_Phase2_Newbridge` | Reach | Phase 2 go-ahead | Kildare, 18+ | Clinic teaser videos (no location clues) | `/walk-in-gp-newbridge/#clinic-news` |

**Rules:** no health-interest targeting; no before/after; no named medicines; captions on; the safety line on symptom
videos; turn comments to "filter" and moderate daily; don't run ads on videos where comments contain people's personal
health details (hide those comments first).

**Budget:** €0–50/month Lean, €100–300 Growth, €300–600 Aggressive (strategy §17). Minimum daily budgets apply per ad group — check in TikTok Ads Manager.
