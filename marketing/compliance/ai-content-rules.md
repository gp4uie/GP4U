# AI content rules

**AI may help with:** ideation, outlines, first drafts, repurposing (article → captions), headline variants, planning,
design concepts, grammar.

**AI must never be used to create:** images or video of doctors, patients, staff or the clinic (fake or "illustrative");
testimonials, reviews or patient stories; statistics or outcomes; medical claims that haven't been checked against a
named source; voice clones of GP4U doctors; content published without human review.

**Always:**
- A human edits every AI-assisted draft into GP4U's voice (see brand guidelines) — it should never read as AI-written.
- Medical content goes through clinical review regardless of who drafted it.
- Real photography first; licensed website photos second; simple typographic templates third. No AI imagery.
- Don't paste patient information, booking data or anything identifiable into AI tools.
- If a platform requires AI labelling for synthetic media, we shouldn't be publishing synthetic media in the first place.
