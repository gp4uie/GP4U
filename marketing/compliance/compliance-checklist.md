# Pre-publish compliance checklist

Tick every line for every post, video, ad, email, article and press release. Anything marked **REVIEW** goes to a
professional (GP, DPO, solicitor, indemnity insurer) — don't guess.

## Truth & claims
- [ ] Every claim is true today and verifiable (hours, services, prices from `/fees/` on the day, "Irish-registered GPs")
- [ ] No superlatives we can't prove: leading, best, No.1, fastest, cheapest, award-winning
- [ ] No "same day", "24/7", "instant" unless operationally guaranteed
- [ ] No promised outcomes; prescriptions/certs/referrals always "where your GP considers it appropriate"
- [ ] No invented statistics, patients, reviews, testimonials, doctors, qualifications or photos
- [ ] Newbridge clinic: described as **opening soon** (Phase 2) — no address, location clues or date until approved

## Medical content
- [ ] Reviewed and signed off by a named GP4U GP (log it)
- [ ] General information, not personal advice — the safety line is present on symptom content
- [ ] Emergency guidance correct and unobtrusive (112/999, ED)
- [ ] No named prescription-only medicines in advertising or promotional posts — **REVIEW** (Irish medicines advertising rules / HPRA)
- [ ] Nothing fear-based, no exploiting anxiety, no "you might have…" diagnosis-by-post
- [ ] Children's content: aimed at parents, general, reviewed; never directed at children; no identifiable children in clinical context

## Professional standards
- [ ] Consistent with the Medical Council *Guide to Professional Conduct and Ethics* (9th ed.) on information and advertising — accurate, evidence-based, not misleading, no unrealistic expectations — **REVIEW** anything borderline
- [ ] Doctors named only with their consent; Medical Council numbers correct if shown
- [ ] Testimonials/patient stories: see [reviews-testimonials-referrals.md](reviews-testimonials-referrals.md) — **REVIEW** before any use

## Privacy (GDPR)
- [ ] No patient information anywhere (screens, paper, reflections, comments, DMs reposted)
- [ ] No health data in URLs, UTMs, pixels, ad audiences or email segments
- [ ] Email only to opted-in addresses; unsubscribe link present
- [ ] Anyone identifiable has signed a release (and patients a separate consent)
- [ ] Comments/DMs with personal health details: hide, reply privately, never discuss care publicly

## Platform rules
- [ ] Google Ads Healthcare & medicines policy (**REVIEW** prescription-related keywords/certification)
- [ ] Meta advertising standards — no personal-attribute wording ("Do you have…?"), no health-based targeting
- [ ] TikTok healthcare ad policy; 18+ targeting only
- [ ] ASAI code (legal, decent, honest, truthful)

## Final
- [ ] Brand check: palette, fonts, "One tap. Real care." used correctly
- [ ] Links work and carry UTMs
- [ ] Approver name + date recorded
