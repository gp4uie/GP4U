# Reviews, testimonials, patient content and referrals

## Never
Fabricated reviews or testimonials · reviews from staff, family or friends posing as patients · buying reviews ·
incentives for reviews (discounts, prizes, entries) · asking only happy patients ("review gating") · editing or
selectively quoting reviews to change their meaning · AI-generated patient stories · replying in a way that confirms
someone is a patient or discusses their care.

## Reviews (once real patients exist)
- **Where:** Google (after the Business Profile is verified — Phase 3), Facebook recommendations. Online-only patients:
  Google reviews can't attach to an unverified profile, so use Facebook until then.
- **How to ask (everyone, the same way):** a line in the post-consultation email or the confirmation page — *"Tell us
  how we did. Your honest feedback helps us improve: [link]. Please don't include medical details."* Frequency: once
  per patient per quarter at most.
- **Replying:** within 2 working days, thank the person, never confirm they are a patient or mention their care, take
  complaints offline: *"Thank you for taking the time. We'd like to understand more — please email admin@gp4u.ie so we
  can look into it properly."*
- **Complaints process:** reviews that describe a clinical concern go to the clinical lead.

## Testimonials & patient stories in marketing
Only if **all** are true: genuinely given; written, specific, revocable consent for that use and channel; no health
details beyond what the patient chose to share; medically appropriate; not misleading about typical outcomes; logged.
**REVIEW before first use** — check the Medical Council guide, ASAI code and your indemnity insurer's view. Until then, use
the doctors' own voices and anonymised, generic process descriptions ("what happens in a consultation"), not patient
stories.

## User-generated content
Reposting a patient's own post requires their written permission and must not reveal health information. Prefer not to.

## Referral programme (future — concept only)
1. **Non-financial first:** "Share GP4U" link after a consultation; family registration at the clinic; employer and
   community information packs (see local outreach).
2. **No money, discounts, credits or gifts** for referring patients until checked against the Medical Council guide,
   Irish consumer and competition law, and with the indemnity insurer — **REVIEW**.
3. Employer arrangements (e.g. corporate accounts) are contracts — legal review; never share individual employees' health
   information with employers.
4. Track referrals only by UTM/referral code on the link — never by patient name.
