# Editorial & clinical review process

Applies to website articles, social posts with any medical information, video scripts, ads and emails.

1. **Brief** — marketing writes the brief (question, audience, sources, CTA).
2. **Draft** — marketing (AI allowed for outline/first draft only; every fact checked against a named source).
3. **Clinical review** — a named GP4U GP checks accuracy, safety-netting, tone, and that nothing implies a diagnosis or
   a guaranteed prescription. They add or correct red flags. **No review = no publish.**
4. **Compliance check** — [../compliance/compliance-checklist.md](../compliance/compliance-checklist.md) (claims, medicines,
   privacy, children, emergency line).
5. **Publish** with byline/reviewer and date (articles); keep a log (sheet: item, reviewer, date, next review).
6. **Review cycle** — articles every 12 months or when guidance changes; social evergreen posts every 6 months.

**Log columns:** ID · Title · Channel · Draft by · Reviewed by (GP name) · Review date · Changes · Published · Next review.
