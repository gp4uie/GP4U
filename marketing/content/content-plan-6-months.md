# 6-month content plan (website articles)

**Quality over quantity: 2 articles a month.** Each one answers a real patient question, is written in plain Irish
English, is **reviewed and signed off by a named GP4U GP** before publishing, shows "Written by / Medically reviewed by
[real name], [date]", links to reputable Irish sources (HSE.ie, Medical Council), and ends with a clear, non-pushy path
to book plus the emergency line. No AI mass production — AI may help with outlines and first drafts only.

Where they live: relaunch the Health Info section (`/blog.html`, currently hidden and noindexed) as `/health-info/` with
proper article pages — a development task (clean URLs, Article + MedicalWebPage schema with reviewer, sitemap). Ask Dev.

## Article brief template
- **Question it answers** (in the patient's words)
- **Who it's for** / what they already know
- **Key facts** (with sources: HSE.ie page, guideline)
- **What GP4U can and can't do** for this
- **Urgent/red-flag section** (always)
- **Internal links** (2–3) and one CTA
- **Reviewer** (name, role, date) · **Next review date** (12 months)
