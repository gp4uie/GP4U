## How GP4U should feel on TikTok
Native, calm, useful. A real GP talking to camera in a real room, one idea per video, captions on, 15–45 seconds.
No dances, no trending sounds that undercut clinical credibility, no fear hooks, no "doctor reacts" to other people's
medical content.

**Formats that fit:** "GP answers" (reply to a genuine comment with a video), "3 things I tell patients about…",
myth vs fact, "what actually happens when you…", behind-the-scenes of building GP4U, the Newbridge clinic journey
(Phase 2, no location clues).

**Rules:** captions on every video; on-screen "General information, not personal medical advice. Emergencies: 112/999"
on any symptom video; never diagnose in comments; don't duet/stitch patients; comments with personal medical details →
kind reply asking them to book or contact us privately (and hide the comment if it exposes their health).

## TikTok ads (Spark Ads)
Boost only organic videos that are already doing well (strong watch-through, saves). Objective: *Reach* or *Video views*
first; *Website traffic* to `/online-gp/` once the pixel has consented data. Audience: Ireland, 18+ (never under-18),
broad — no interest targeting by health condition. Budget: see strategy §17.
