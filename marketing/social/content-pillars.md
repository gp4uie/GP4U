# Content pillars

Aim for roughly this mix over any month (Phase 1). Phase 2 raises "Future clinic" to ~15%.

| # | Pillar | Share | Purpose | Examples | Guardrails |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Meet GP4U** | 10% | Who we are, why we exist, what we offer | Founder story, "why One tap. Real care.", what GP4U does/doesn't do | Real people only; no invented bios |
| 2 | **GP education** | 30% | Useful, shareable, trust-building | When to see a GP; urgent warning signs; winter bugs; hay fever; coughs; sore throats; skin; headaches; women's/men's health; common childhood illnesses (for parents) | Clinically reviewed; general info not advice; safety-net line; no fear |
| 3 | **Online GP explained** | 20% | Remove friction to booking | How booking works; what happens in a consultation; what you need; prescriptions to your pharmacy; sick certs; prices | Only true process; prices from the live site |
| 4 | **Behind GP4U** | 10% | Humanise | Team, building the service, a day in the life, tech/privacy decisions | No patient data/screens in shot |
| 5 | **Patient convenience** | 10% | Relatable moments | Busy weeks, work, travel, family life, evenings/weekends (7 days online) | Don't promise instant access |
| 6 | **Myth vs fact** | 10% | Correct misconceptions, invite shares | "Antibiotics cure colds", "Online GPs can't prescribe", "You need an appointment for everything" | Sourced facts; reviewed |
| 7 | **Future clinic** (Phase 2) | 5% → 15% | Build local anticipation | "Something new is coming to Newbridge", renovation details, team prep | **No address, no location clues, no date** until approved |
| 8 | **Brand content** | 5% | Emotional brand memory | "One tap. Real care." moments; seasonal messages | Not salesy |

**CTA ladder:** education posts → "Save this" / "Share with someone who needs it"; explainers → "See how it works at
gp4u.ie"; conversion posts (≤ 1 in 4) → "Book an online GP — link in bio"; clinic → "Get opening news".
