# Video scripts

Written to be **said**, not read — a real GP talking to one person. Rough word counts: 15 s ≈ 35 words, 30 s ≈ 75,
45 s ≈ 110, 60 s ≈ 150. Change the wording to how you actually talk; keep the facts. **GP review before filming.**

Structure every time: **HOOK → USEFUL INFORMATION → TRUST / HUMAN → CTA**. Don't end every video with the slogan — use
it on about 1 in 3, and let the end card carry it the rest of the time.

On-screen for any symptom video: *General information, not personal medical advice. Emergencies: call 112 or 999.*

---

## Reusable templates

### 15 seconds
- **Hook (0–3 s):** a question the viewer is already asking. "Can you see a GP online in Ireland?"
- **Info (3–10 s):** one fact. "Yes — by video or phone, with an Irish-registered GP."
- **Trust (10–13 s):** one human detail. "You'll see the price before you book."
- **CTA (13–15 s):** "Link's in our bio."

### 30 seconds
- **Hook (0–3 s)** — relatable situation.
- **Info (3–20 s)** — 2–3 short points, one idea each.
- **Trust (20–26 s)** — what a GP would honestly add ("and if it needs a look in person, I'll tell you").
- **CTA (26–30 s)** — save / share / book.

### 45 seconds
- **Hook (0–4 s)** · **Context (4–12 s)** — why this matters · **3 points (12–35 s)** · **Safety net (35–40 s)** —
  when it's urgent · **CTA (40–45 s)**.

### 60-second educational
- **Hook (0–4 s)** · **What it is (4–15 s)** · **What helps (15–30 s)** · **When to see a GP (30–45 s)** · **When it's an
  emergency (45–52 s)** · **Human close + CTA (52–60 s)**.

## Ready-to-film scripts

### V01 — One tap. Real care. Why we built GP4U (45 s)
**HOOK:** "I'm Dr ___, a GP. And I built GP4U because getting to see a GP shouldn't be the hard part."
**INFO:** "Most people who need a GP don't need an ambulance — they need a sensible conversation with a doctor, at a time
that works. So with GP4U you can see an Irish-registered GP by video or phone, seven days a week, from wherever you are in
Ireland. You see the price before you book."
**TRUST:** "It's still real care. I'll listen, I'll explain, and if you need to be seen in person — or it's an
emergency — I'll tell you straight."
**CTA:** "One tap. Real care. That's GP4U."

### V02 — What happens during an online GP consultation? (45 s)
**HOOK:** "Never had an online GP appointment? Here's exactly what happens."
**INFO:** "When you book, you answer a few short questions, so I know what's going on before we talk. At your time, you
tap the link on your confirmation page and join — video or phone. We talk it through, like any GP visit. Then I explain
what I think, and what happens next. That might be advice, a prescription sent to the pharmacy you picked, a cert, or a
referral letter — if it's appropriate."
**TRUST:** "And sometimes the honest answer is 'you need to be examined in person.' I'll tell you that too."
**CTA:** "Book online — link in our bio."

### V03 — Can you see a GP online in Ireland? (15 s)
**HOOK:** "Can you see a GP online in Ireland?"
**INFO:** "Yes. By video or phone, seven days a week, with an Irish-registered GP."
**TRUST:** "You'll see the price before you book."
**CTA:** "Link's in our bio. One tap. Real care."

### V05 — When should a cough be checked? (45 s)
**HOOK:** "Had a cough for weeks and wondering if it's worth getting checked?"
**CONTEXT:** "Most coughs after a cold settle on their own, but they can hang around for up to three weeks."
**POINTS:** "Speak to a GP if your cough has lasted more than three weeks; if you're coughing up blood; if you're short of
breath or have chest pain; or if you've lost weight without trying, or have a fever that won't settle."
**SAFETY NET:** "If you're struggling to breathe, that's an emergency — call 112 or 999."
**CTA:** "Save this for cough season — and if you're worried, talk to a GP."

### V06 — Online GP vs walk-in GP (45 s)
**HOOK:** "Online GP or in person — which do you actually need?"
**POINTS:** "Online works well for lots of everyday things: advice, many infections, contraception reviews, repeat
prescriptions, sick certs. In person is better when you need to be examined — listening to your chest properly, looking
in an ear, checking an injury."
**TRUST:** "If you book online and I think you need to be seen, I'll tell you. And our new walk-in GP clinic in Newbridge is
opening soon for exactly that."  ← *(Phase 2 wording; Phase 3: "…and you can walk into our Newbridge clinic.")*
**CTA:** "Not sure? Book online and we'll point you the right way."

### V09 — What to have ready (30 s)
**HOOK:** "Before your online GP appointment, have these ready."
**INFO:** "Somewhere private and quiet. A list of any medicines you take. The name of your pharmacy. Your phone charged —
and good light if it's something I might need to look at, like a rash."
**TRUST:** "That's it. The rest is just a normal conversation with your GP."
**CTA:** "Save this for next time."

### V10 — GP, pharmacy or 112? (45 s)
**HOOK:** "Not sure whether you need a pharmacy, a GP or an ambulance? Quick guide."
**POINTS:** "Your pharmacist is brilliant for minor things and over-the-counter advice. A GP — online or in person — is for
symptoms that aren't improving, new problems, prescriptions and certs. And 112 or 999 is for emergencies: chest pain,
severe difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, collapse, a seizure, or a severe allergic reaction."
**TRUST:** "If you're not sure — ask. That's what we're here for."
**CTA:** "Share this with your family group chat."

### V11 — Myth: antibiotics fix colds (20 s)
**HOOK:** "Will antibiotics help my cold? Short answer: no."
**INFO:** "Colds are caused by viruses, and antibiotics only work on bacteria. Taking them when you don't need them can
give you side effects and makes resistance worse."
**TRUST:** "When you do need them, your GP will prescribe them."
**CTA:** "Follow for more myth-busting."

### V13 — How prescriptions reach your pharmacy (20 s)
**HOOK:** "Where does your prescription go after an online consultation?"
**INFO:** "If your GP decides a prescription is right for you, it's sent to the pharmacy you chose when you booked. You
collect it and pay for the medicine there, as usual."
**CTA:** "See how it works at gp4u.ie."

### V36 — When online isn't the right choice (30 s)
**HOOK:** "Sometimes I'll tell you an online consultation isn't enough."
**INFO:** "Some problems need hands-on examination, and some need tests. If that's the case, I'll explain why and where to
go. And anything like chest pain or severe difficulty breathing — don't book anything, call 112 or 999."
**TRUST:** "Being honest about that is part of real care."
**CTA:** "One tap. Real care."

## Production checklist
- Vertical 1080×1920, 25/30 fps, natural light facing a window, lapel mic.
- Plain, real background (clinic room or tidy office) — no fake props, no stethoscope as decoration.
- No patient information visible anywhere; demo booking only for screen recordings.
- Captions burned in (check Irish place names and medical terms).
- End card 3 s: cream, badge, "One tap. Real care.", www.gp4u.ie.
- File name: `gp4u-v05-cough-3-weeks-2026-10.mp4`.
