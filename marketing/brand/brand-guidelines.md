# GP4U brand guidelines

## Core

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | **GP4U** (always this way — not "Gp4u", "GP 4 U" or "GP4You") |
| Primary slogan | **One tap. Real care.** |
| Supporting line | Online GP care from wherever you are in Ireland. |
| Clinic line (Phase 2–3) | **GP care when you need it.** |
| Brand idea | Real doctors. Real care. Made easier. |
| Emotional centre | Convenience without compromising trust. Technology without losing the human doctor. |

## Personality

Human · professional · trustworthy · modern · Irish · accessible · clinically credible · convenient · warm · premium.

**We are not:** a generic telemedicine startup, an "AI healthcare" company, a cheap prescription shop, a faceless
corporate platform, or a stock-photo website.

## Voice

Write like a good Irish GP talking to a patient: plain, calm, kind, specific.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "See a GP online by video or phone." | "Revolutionary digital healthcare ecosystem" |
| "Where your GP thinks it's appropriate" | "Get your prescription guaranteed" |
| "If it's an emergency, call 112 or 999." | Fear: "Don't ignore these deadly signs!" |
| "Book online and pick a time that suits you." | "Ireland's fastest / best / leading GP" (unverifiable) |
| Irish spelling and context (GP, A&E / ED, pharmacy, sick cert, HSE, medical card) | US terms (ER, drugstore, primary care physician) |

- Second person ("you"), short sentences, active voice.
- Numbers only when true and sourced (prices from the live site; never invented stats).
- Always say what online care **can't** do when it matters: some problems need an in-person examination.
- Emergency line, where relevant, kept clear but unobtrusive: *For emergencies, call 112 or 999 or go to your nearest
  Emergency Department.*

## Colour (from the website — do not change)

| Teal deep | `#0A4D4D` | Primary dark, top bar, headings on light |

| Ink | `#12303A` | Body headings, main text |
| Muted | `#4D646D` | Secondary text |
| Cream | `#FBF7F0` | Page / card background |
| Sand | `#F3ECDF` | Alternate sections |

Contrast: body text ink/muted on cream passes WCAG AA; white text only on teal-deep or teal.

## Typography

- **Fraunces** (600) — headlines, the slogan. Serif warmth = "real care".
- **Manrope** (500–800) — everything else. Clean, modern = "one tap".
- Social graphics: headline 64–96px Fraunces; body 26–34px Manrope; never more than two sizes of body text per graphic.

## Logo

- The pulse mark on the dark rounded badge (`public/img/gp4u-icon-bright.svg`) + "GP4U" in Manrope 800, teal deep.
- Clear space = the badge's height ÷ 2. Minimum badge size 32px digital.
- Don't recolour the pulse, stretch, add effects, or place the badge on busy photos without a cream panel.

## "One tap. Real care." as a recurring device

- End card of every video (3 s): cream background, badge + "One tap. Real care." + www.gp4u.ie.
- Bottom strip on static posts (teal bar, cream text) — see [../social/design-templates/](../social/design-templates/).
- Spoken sign-off by the GP, sparingly — not every video (it should feel like a line, not a jingle).

## Imagery

Real people, real rooms, natural light. See [photography-brief.md](photography-brief.md). Until the shoot happens, use
the current licensed website photos and simple typographic graphics — **never** AI-generated people or clinics.
