# GP4U photography & video brief

**Purpose:** build a library of real GP4U images that replaces stock photography on the website, social, ads, PR and
the clinic launch. This library is a core brand asset.

## Feel

Natural light, calm, warm, unhurried. Irish homes and a real Newbridge clinic. People who look like our patients and
doctors, doing real things. Premium through restraint — clean frames, cream/teal/wood tones, no clutter.

**Avoid:** generic stock doctors, big posed smiles to camera, stethoscopes as props, lab coats nobody wears, blue
"medical" filters, fake screens, AI imagery, anything that looks staged or clinical-cold.

## Shot list

### A. Online GP (shoot now — Phase 1)
1. GP at desk on a video consultation (screen shows only a blurred/actor patient; never real patient data)
2. Close-ups: hands on laptop/phone, headset, notepad — no readable clinical info
3. Patient side (actor/model with release): kitchen table, car park at work, sofa with a sleeping toddler nearby, evening lamp light
4. Phone in hand showing the GP4U booking screen (use a demo booking, not real data)
5. Pharmacy pick-up moment (only with the pharmacy's written permission; no pharmacy branding unless agreed)

### B. People
6. Individual portraits of each doctor (environmental + clean head-and-shoulders), with their written consent
7. Team together, informal
8. Behind the scenes: planning, laptop work, a coffee, a laugh — real moments

### C. Newbridge clinic (Phase 2 = detail only; Phase 3 = everything)
9. **Phase 2 (no location clues):** renovation details — paint swatches, new chair being unwrapped, light through a window, a hand fixing a sign that says only "GP4U" — **no street, shopfront, signage with address, Eircode, landmarks or car registrations**
10. Phase 3: exterior with signage, reception, waiting area, consultation room, accessibility features, parking/entrance
11. Phase 3: first patients (actors or consenting patients only; never during a real consultation)

### D. Video b-roll (for Reels/TikTok)
12. Laptop opening, booking tap, confirmation screen (demo data), kettle, door opening, clinic light switching on (Phase 2)

## Rules

- **Consent:** signed model release for every identifiable person; separate written consent for any patient (see
  [../compliance/reviews-testimonials-referrals.md](../compliance/reviews-testimonials-referrals.md)). Children only with parent/guardian consent and never in a clinical context.
- **No real patient information** on any screen, paper, whiteboard or monitor in frame.
- **Address protection (until launch approval):** nothing that identifies the clinic's location.
- Deliver: RAW + edited JPEG (sRGB), web versions at 2000px long edge; vertical 1080×1920 crops for Stories/Reels; file
  names like `gp4u-online-gp-consultation-desk-01.jpg` (descriptive, lowercase, hyphenated).
- Usage rights: unlimited, perpetual, all media, including paid ads.

## Priority order
Doctor portraits → online consultation set → behind the scenes → clinic details (Phase 2) → full clinic (Phase 3).
