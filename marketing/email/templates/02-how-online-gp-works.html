How seeing a GP online works
  • Choose a service — video, phone, repeat prescription or sick cert.
  • Tell us what's going on — a short, private questionnaire so your GP is prepared.
  • Pick a time and pay securely — the price is shown before you pay.
  • Speak to your GP — join from the link on your confirmation page.

Where your GP considers it appropriate, prescriptions go to the pharmacy you choose, and sick certs and referral letters are issued securely. If you need to be examined in person, your GP will tell you.
