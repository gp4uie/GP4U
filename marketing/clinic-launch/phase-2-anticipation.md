# PHASE 2 — Building anticipation: "Something new is coming to Newbridge."

**Status:** ready; start only when the owner says go. **Rules:** no address, no Eircode, no street/shopfront/landmark/
signage/car-reg in any photo or video, no opening date until confirmed, never "open now".

## Goal
Build a local audience in Newbridge/Kildare (followers + email sign-ups) before opening, so launch day has people waiting.
The website already has the sign-up (`/walk-in-gp-newbridge/#clinic-news`) and "opening soon" wording everywhere.

## Content (1–2 pieces a week across IG/FB/TikTok; Facebook first for local reach)
| Week | Piece | Visual | Copy idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Teaser | Template `clinic-teaser` | "Something new is coming to Newbridge. 👀 Be first to know — link in bio." |
| 2 | Renovation detail | Paint swatches / materials (no windows) | "Choosing colours. Warm, calm, not your average waiting room." |
| 3 | Why Newbridge | Founder to camera (indoors, neutral background) | "Why we chose Newbridge" — personal, no address |
| 4 | Team preparation | Hands, training, equipment unboxing (non-identifying) | "Getting ready." |
| 5 | What the clinic will offer | Carousel | "Walk-in GP care, no appointment needed once we open; family GP registration." |
| 6 | Ask the community | Poll / question | "What matters most to you in a GP clinic?" (read answers; don't collect health details) |
| 7+ | Countdown only after a date is confirmed | — | Switch to Phase 3 plan |

**Local amplification (organic):** share to the GP4U Facebook page; ask to share in local community groups only where
group rules allow business posts; no paid "local" ads until the owner approves Phase 2 spend (`META_Phase2_Newbridge`).

**Measure:** clinic email sign-ups (admin → signups count), Kildare share of followers (Instagram/Facebook insights),
engagement on clinic posts.
