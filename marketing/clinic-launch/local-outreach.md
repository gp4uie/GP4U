# Local community outreach — Newbridge & Kildare (Phase 3; light-touch Phase 2)

**Principles:** build real relationships; never imply endorsement or partnership until one exists in writing; no
payments/incentives for referrals; general health information only (no clinics in schools, no targeting children);
respect GDPR — don't collect health information at events.

## Who (build your own list — no invented contacts)
| Type | Examples to research | Offer |
|---|---|---|
| Community groups | Tidy Towns, residents' associations, active retirement groups, parent & toddler groups | Talk: "When to see a GP / pharmacy / A&E"; notice-board poster |
| Sports clubs | GAA, soccer, rugby, athletics clubs | First-aid-season article for newsletters; notice board; **no** sponsorship implying medical cover |
| Employers | Larger Newbridge/Kildare employers, business parks, Chamber | Staff info pack: online GP 7 days + walk-in clinic |
| Pharmacies | Local independent pharmacies | Introduce GP4U; how online prescriptions reach them; clinic hours |
| Schools / colleges | Only on invitation | General health talks for parents/staff |
| Local media | Kildare newspapers and radio | Launch story, expert seasonal comment |
| Events | Local festivals, markets | Information stand (no health data collection) |

## Templates

### Email — community group
Subject: A new GP service for Newbridge — happy to help your members
> Hi [name],
> I'm [name], [role] at GP4U. We're opening a walk-in GP clinic in Newbridge [on DATE / soon], and we already offer
> online GP consultations across Ireland.
> We'd love to be useful to [group] — for example a short, practical talk on when to see a GP, pharmacist or A&E, or a
> poster for your notice board. No sales pitch.
> Would that be of interest? Happy to work around you.
> [name] · GP4U · www.gp4u.ie · [phone once published]

### Email — employer

> could I send it over or drop in? [Any corporate arrangement to be agreed separately and checked for compliance.]
