# PR kit

## Angles
1. **New Irish GP service** — founder story: a practising GP building a service to make GP access easier (national/regional).
2. **Online GP access** — practical piece: when online consultations help and when they don't (consumer/health press).
3. **New clinic for Newbridge** — local jobs, local access, walk-in without appointment (Kildare press/radio) — Phase 3.
4. **Expert comment** — seasonal advice from a named GP4U GP (winter bugs, hay fever) — ongoing.
5. **Healthcare trade/LinkedIn** — building a modern, privacy-first GP service.

All claims must be true and verifiable; no statistics unless sourced; the GP quoted must approve every quote.

## Press release template
**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE** / **EMBARGOED UNTIL [date time]**

**[Headline — factual, e.g. "New walk-in GP clinic opens in Newbridge"]**
*[Sub-head — e.g. "GP4U clinic adds in-person care to its online GP service"]*

**[Town], [date]** — [One-paragraph summary: who, what, where, when, why.]

[Paragraph 2 — what patients get: walk-in without appointment during [hours]; online consultations 7 days; family
registration; fees [link].]

"[Quote from founder GP — why, in their own words]," said Dr [Name], [role], GP4U.

[Paragraph 3 — local context: jobs created (if true), opening event (if any), accessibility.]

[Paragraph 4 — online service: Irish-registered GPs, video or phone, prices shown before booking.]

**Ends.**

**Notes to editors:** About GP4U (80 words) · Photos available (real, credited) · Interviews: Dr [Name] ·
Contact: [name, email, phone] · www.gp4u.ie

## Media pitch (email, 120 words)
Subject: [Local angle] — e.g. "Newbridge gets a walk-in GP clinic — interview with the GP behind it"
> Hi [journalist first name], I noticed your piece on [their recent relevant story]. [One sentence news hook.] Dr [Name],
> a practising GP, set up GP4U to [reason]. [One concrete, verifiable detail.] Would you like an interview or photos
> for [date]? I can also offer [practical angle, e.g. "what to do when you can't get a GP appointment"]. Thanks, [name]

## Founder story (to be written by the founder — prompts)
Why medicine/general practice? · What frustrated you about access? · A (non-identifiable) moment that made you build
GP4U · What "real care" means · Why Newbridge · What you'll never compromise on.

## Clinic launch story (Phase 3)
Opening day photos (consenting people) · first-day facts (true) · what's different · community involvement.

## Journalist outreach list — structure (fill with real, researched contacts only)
`name, outlet, beat/section, region, email (public/professional), recent relevant article (URL), angle to pitch, last contacted, response, notes`
