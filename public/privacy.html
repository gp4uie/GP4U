Privacy & GDPR Notice

Data controller: GP4U Clinic, Newbridge, Co. Kildare (contact: admin@gp4u.ie)

This notice covers patients seen in person at our walk-in clinic and family practice as well as patients who use our online consultation service.

What we collect

When you register as a patient, book in for our walk-in clinic, or book an online consultation we collect your name, date of birth, contact details, and the health information you provide in your intake questionnaire and during your consultation. This is "special category" data under GDPR, and we only process it to provide you with medical care, which is a lawful basis under Article 9(2)(h) (health care provided by a health professional).

How we use it

  • To arrange and carry out your consultation
  • To keep a clinical record, as required by Medical Council guidance
  • To process payment (via Stripe — Stripe does not receive your health information, only payment details)
  • To respond to messages you send us about your care

How long we keep it

Medical records are retained in line with Medical Council of Ireland guidance (currently a minimum of 8 years for adult patients; longer for children — until age 25, or 26 if a note was made at 17). Update this section to match your own retention policy once confirmed.

Your rights

You can request a copy of everything we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data, by emailing admin@gp4u.ie. Every request is reviewed by a person rather than actioned automatically, since GPs are legally required to retain clinical records for a minimum period (see "How long we keep it" above) — we will provide or delete what can legally be provided or deleted, and respond within one month. You have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) at any time.

Security

Video consultations and messages are transmitted over encrypted connections, and the site itself is only served over HTTPS. Clinical notes, prescriptions, sick cert/referral letter details, and your intake answers are also encrypted at rest in our database. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe — GP4U never sees or stores your card details.

Cookies

This website uses one strictly necessary cookie (a session cookie) to keep you signed in when you log in as a patient or member of staff. It is deleted when you log out or it expires, and it is not used for tracking or advertising.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Two third parties are involved when you use the site: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (which receives your IP address to deliver them), and if you pay for an online consultation you are taken to Stripe's secure payment page, which sets its own cookies to process your payment.

Terms of Use

GP4U is intended for non-emergency GP consultations. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 112/999 or attend your nearest Emergency Department immediately. Consultations are provided by GPs registered with the Irish Medical Council. In some cases, your GP may advise that an in-person examination is required and decline to treat remotely — this is for your safety.
