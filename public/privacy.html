Privacy & GDPR Notice

Data controller: GP4U (contact: gp4u.ie@gmail.com)

What we collect

When you book a consultation we collect your name, date of birth, contact details, and the health information you provide in your intake questionnaire and during your consultation. This is "special category" data under GDPR, and we only process it to provide you with medical care, which is a lawful basis under Article 9(2)(h) (health care provided by a health professional).

How we use it

  • To arrange and carry out your consultation
  • To keep a clinical record, as required by Medical Council guidance
  • To process payment (via Stripe — Stripe does not receive your health information, only payment details)
  • To respond to messages you send us about your care

How long we keep it

Medical records are retained in line with Medical Council of Ireland guidance (currently a minimum of 8 years for adult patients; longer for children — until age 25, or 26 if a note was made at 17). Update this section to match your own retention policy once confirmed.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask questions about how it's used, by emailing gp4u.ie@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Security

Video consultations and messages are transmitted over encrypted connections. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe — GP4U never sees or stores your card details.

Terms of Use

GP4U is intended for non-emergency GP consultations. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 112/999 or attend your nearest Emergency Department immediately. Consultations are provided by GPs registered with the Irish Medical Council. In some cases, your GP may advise that an in-person examination is required and decline to treat remotely — this is for your safety.

GP4U — Not for use in a medical emergency.
