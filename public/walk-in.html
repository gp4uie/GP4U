Walk-In Clinic

See a GP in Newbridge without an appointment. Come in any day of the week during opening hours.

How it works

1
Come in

Drop in at any time during opening hours. There's no need to book.

2
Check in at reception

Give our team your details and let them know why you're here.

3
See a doctor

A GP will assess you and, where needed, arrange prescriptions, sick certs or referrals.

What to bring

A list of any medicines you take, and anything relevant such as recent test results or letters from other doctors.

Walk-in is a good choice for

Everyday health problems that need to be seen today.

  • Coughs, colds, flu and chest infections
  • Sore throats and ear problems
  • Urine infections
  • Rashes and skin infections
  • Minor injuries and sprains
  • Stomach upsets and vomiting
  • Contraception and emergency contraception
  • Sick certificates and medical letters

When to go to A&E instead

Call 112 or 999, or go to your nearest Emergency Department, if you or someone else has:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Signs of a stroke — face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Heavy bleeding that won't stop
  • A seizure or loss of consciousness
  • A severe allergic reaction
  • A serious injury or suspected broken bone with deformity
  • Thoughts of harming yourself — call 112 or 999, or Samaritans on 116 123

Can't get to the clinic? See a GP online.

Video and audio consultations, repeat prescriptions and sick certs — book in minutes and speak to an Irish-registered GP from home.
