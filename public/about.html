About GP4U

Built by a GP. Designed around patients.

GP4U was set up by a practising Irish GP to make everyday GP care more convenient — without losing the careful, personal approach of a good family doctor.

Who we are

One service, three ways to get care

Walk-in clinic

See a GP in Newbridge, Co. Kildare without an appointment, seven days a week.

Comprehensive family practice

Register yourself and your family for continuing care, from children to older adults.

Online GP

Video and phone consultations, repeat prescriptions and certificates from home.

Medical leadership

Led by a practising GP

GP4U is led by a practising Irish GP. Every consultation is carried out by a doctor registered with the Medical Council of Ireland.

  • Registered doctors

    Doctors practise on the register of the Medical Council of Ireland.

  • Patient first

    Clear explanations, sensible advice and honest recommendations — including when you need to be seen in person.

  • Private & secure

    Your health information is encrypted and handled in line with GDPR. Privacy notice

Our approach

Why GP4U was created

To give patients a simpler way to see a GP — in person or online — with the personal, careful approach of a good family doctor.

What patients can expect

How your care works

1

Choose how you'd like to be seen

Book an online GP consultation, or walk in at the clinic — no appointment required.

2

Be seen by a registered GP

Your GP listens, assesses you, and explains their advice. If you need to be examined in person, they'll tell you.

3

Follow-up where needed

Prescriptions, certificates or referral letters are issued where clinically appropriate, and online patients can message their GP securely.
