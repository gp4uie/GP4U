About GP4U

GP4U is a telemedicine service built by a practising Irish GP, aimed at making everyday GP care more convenient — without losing the personal, careful approach of a good family doctor. Every consultation is carried out by a GP registered with the Irish Medical Council.

Prefer to be seen in person? GP4U Clinic is a walk-in clinic and family practice on George Street, Newbridge, Co. Kildare — no appointment needed. Walk-in details · Opening hours & directions
