Good to know

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-in patients are seen without an appointment during opening hours. You can also book in online so we know you're on your way.

Can I register my whole family?

Yes. We're a comprehensive family practice caring for adults and children. Use the registration form and add each family member.

What should I bring?

A list of any medicines you take, plus any recent letters or test results from other doctors.

What if it's an emergency?

Call 112 or 999, or go to your nearest Emergency Department. GP4U Clinic is not an emergency service.

Can I see a GP without coming in?

Yes. Our online consultations offer video and audio appointments, repeat prescriptions and sick certificates.
