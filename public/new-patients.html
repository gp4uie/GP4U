Questions about registering

Can I register my children and family?

Yes. Add each family member (name, date of birth and relationship) at the bottom of the form. You can share their health details when you first see us.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our team reviews your details and gets in touch. You'll also receive a confirmation email with your reference number.

Need to be seen today?

You don't need to register online first — book in for our walk-in clinic or just come in. If it's an emergency, call 112 or 999.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Your address and health details are encrypted at rest, and only our clinical team can view them. See our Privacy & GDPR Notice.
